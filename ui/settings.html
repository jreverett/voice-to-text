Welcome to Voice-to-Text

Hold your hotkey, speak, and the text is pasted where you're typing. Transcription uses Groq by default — fast, cloud-based, and free with your own API key.

Set up Groq (recommended)

  1. Open the Groq console and sign in with Google or email — no credit card needed.
  2. Create an API key and copy it.
  3. Paste it below and click Save.

or

Use Local Whisper (offline)

No API key, nothing leaves your machine. Downloads a ~466 MB model on the next launch, and transcription is slower and a little less accurate than Groq.
